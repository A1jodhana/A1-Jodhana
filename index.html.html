Who We Are

A Jodhpur-rooted team that plans journeys across Rajasthan & India

A1-Jodhana Tours and Travels was founded in the shadow of Mehrangarh Fort with a simple promise: plan every trip the way we would for our own family. What began as a small taxi service for pilgrims and traders visiting the Blue City has grown into a full-service travel company, still headquartered in Jodhpur, now coordinating tours across every major city in Rajasthan and India.

Our strength is local knowledge paired with a genuinely large fleet. Whether you need a quiet sedan for two, a luxury tempo traveller for a wedding party, or a 52-seater coach for a school pilgrimage, our Jodhpur dispatch desk and our partner drivers in Jaipur, Udaipur, Jaisalmer, Delhi, Haridwar and beyond keep every itinerary running on time.

Sacred Himalayan Circuit

Char Dham Yatra

Yamunotri, Gangotri, Kedarnath and Badrinath — planned from Haridwar / Rishikesh with comfortable stays, verified drivers familiar with hill routes, and support for the trek/pony/palki legs.

The Four Sacred Shrines

The Chota Char Dham circuit begins at Haridwar or Rishikesh and covers, in order, Yamunotri (source of the Yamuna), Gangotri (source of the Ganges), Kedarnath (a jyotirlinga of Shiva) and Badrinath (dedicated to Vishnu) — all in the Garhwal Himalayas of Uttarakhand. We handle vehicle transfers, hotel/dormitory bookings, and can arrange pony, palki or helicopter add-ons for the trekking sections at Kedarnath and Yamunotri.
